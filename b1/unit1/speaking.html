🎤 SPEAKING EXERCISE · UNIT 01

Life Highlights

Two fun options — choose one and go! Both practise the present perfect and Unit 1 vocabulary.

THE TASK

🎯 Two Truths and a Wish

Stand up and speak to the class for at least 1 minute. You will say three sentences using the present perfect:

  • Two truths — things you have really done in your life.
  • One wish — something you have never done but really want to try.

After you speak, the class votes on which one is the wish. If they can't guess, you win a point! 🏆

EXAMPLE

MARIA — 1 MIN
Maria:"Hi everyone. Today I'm going to share three things about my life — but only two are true. See if you can find the wish!"
Maria:"Number one: I have travelled to Mexico three times, and I have eaten real tacos in Guadalajara. The food was amazing."
Maria:"Number two: I have graduated from university with a degree in engineering. It took me five long years, but it was worth it."
Maria:"Number three: I have jumped out of an airplane! I did it in Cartagena and it was the most exciting day of my life."
Maria:"So — which one is the wish? Who thinks it's number one? Number two? Number three? OK, let's see… the answer is number three! I have never jumped from an airplane, but I really want to!"

THE TASK

🎭 The Weirdest Interview Ever

In pairs (2 students only). Choose a ridiculous job from the list below and role-play a job interview for it. One student is the interviewer, the other is the candidate.

The interviewer must ask at least 3 questions using the present perfect. The candidate must answer with the present perfect, using experience — real or invented — that makes them perfect for this crazy job.

Total time: 3–4 minutes per pair. Have fun with it! 🎉

PICK A JOB

🌮 TACO TASTERProfessional food critic for a taco chain. Must love spicy food.
👻 GHOST HUNTERInvestigate haunted houses. Must not be afraid of the dark.
🐕 DOG PSYCHOLOGISTTherapy for dogs with emotional problems.
🎢 THEME PARK SCREAMERTest rollercoasters. Must scream convincingly.
🍦 ICE CREAM DESIGNERInvent new flavors. Must have wild imagination.
🎂 PROFESSIONAL BIRTHDAY GUESTAttend parties for people with no friends.

EXAMPLE — PROFESSIONAL TACO TASTER

JULIÁN (INTERVIEWER) & ANDREA (CANDIDATE) — 3 MIN
Julián:Good morning Andrea! So, you have applied for the position of Professional Taco Taster. Tell me — have you ever eaten more than ten tacos in one day?
Andrea:Yes, I have! Last Tuesday I have eaten… sorry, I ate fifteen tacos in a taco festival. My stomach was very happy.
Julián:Have you ever worked with really spicy food? Some of our tacos are extremely hot.
Andrea:Oh yes, I have tried the ghost pepper three times, and I haven't cried once. Well… only a little bit.
Julián:Interesting. And have you ever graduated from a culinary school?
Andrea:No, I haven't graduated from a school, but I have studied Mexican food for five years — with my grandmother. She is the best taco chef in the world!
Julián:Perfect. One last question: have you ever said "I don't like tacos"?
Andrea:Never! I have never said that in my whole life. Tacos are life!
Julián:You're hired! Welcome to the team. 🌮

USEFUL QUESTIONS FOR THE INTERVIEWER

  • Have you ever worked in…?
  • Have you ever done anything like this before?
  • How long have you been interested in…?
  • What experience have you had with…?
  • Have you studied anything related to…?
  • Have you ever had a strange job?
