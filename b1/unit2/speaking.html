🎤 SPEAKING EXERCISE · UNIT 02

Culture Shocks & Package Mysteries

Two fun options — one individual, one in pairs. Both practise present perfect progressive and Unit 2 vocabulary.

THE TASK

🌍 Culture Shock Confessional

Imagine you have been living in a very different country for the last 3 months. Stand up and speak to the class for at least 1 minute about your experience.

You must include:

  • Which country you have been living in.
  • Three things you have been doing or have been trying (using present perfect progressive).
  • One thing that has shocked or confused you.
  • One thing you have been enjoying.

EXAMPLE

LUIS — 1 MIN — LIVING IN JAPAN
"Hi everyone! For the last three months I have been living in Tokyo, Japan, and honestly, my mind is exploding every day. So, first, I have been trying to eat with chopsticks and I still drop half my food on the table — it's embarrassing!

Second, I have been learning Japanese from a phone app, but people talk SO fast that I only understand three words. Third, I have been taking the subway every day, and I still get lost — the maps look like spaghetti.

What has really shocked me is that people are so quiet on the train — nobody speaks, nobody laughs, everyone just looks at their phone. In Colombia, the bus is like a party!

But what I have been enjoying the most is the food. I have been eating ramen almost every day, and I never get tired of it. Life is good. 🍜"

🇯🇵 JAPANChopsticks, quiet trains, karaoke, ramen, bowing.
🇮🇸 ICELANDCold, geysers, no darkness in summer, fermented shark food.
🇰🇷 SOUTH KOREAK-pop, spicy food, working late, taking your shoes off inside.
🇮🇳 INDIAColours, spicy food, traffic, weddings, saris.
🇫🇮 FINLANDSaunas, silent people, snow, coffee everywhere.
🇦🇺 AUSTRALIASpiders the size of a plate, beaches, weird slang, kangaroos.

USEFUL SENTENCE STARTERS

  • "I have been living in ______ for three months and…"
  • "I have been trying to ______ but it's really hard."
  • "People here have been so ______ (kind / strange / quiet)."
  • "I have been eating ______ almost every day."
  • "What has really shocked me is ______."
  • "I still haven't gotten used to ______."

THE TASK

📦 The Suspicious Package

In pairs (2 students only). Role-play a scene at the post office. One student is the customer with a very weird package to send. The other is the postal clerk asking suspicious questions.

The clerk must ask at least 3 questions with present perfect progressive. The customer must give creative answers (real or invented!) using both present perfect and present perfect progressive.

Total time: 3–4 minutes per pair. Have fun with it! 📦

PICK A STRANGE PACKAGE

🐢 A LIVE TURTLEYou need to send it to your grandmother. It's her birthday gift.
🧦 50 PAIRS OF SOCKSAll old. All smelly. All must arrive in Japan by Friday.
👗 A WEDDING DRESSFor an ex-boyfriend's new wedding. You are "being kind."
🥔 200 POTATOESFrom Boyacá. For a distant cousin who "misses the taste."
📱 15 BROKEN PHONESYou "have been collecting them" and want to send them to a repair guru.
🎂 A HALF-EATEN BIRTHDAY CAKETo your best friend who couldn't come to your party.

EXAMPLE — A LIVE TURTLE

CAMILA (CUSTOMER) & ANDRÉS (CLERK) — 3 MIN
Andrés:Good morning! How can I help you?
Camila:Hi! I'd like to send this package to my grandmother in Barranquilla, please.
Andrés:Sure, let me weigh it… wait — it's moving. What have you been putting in this box?
Camila:Uhm… well, I have been keeping a turtle at home for two years, and today is my grandmother's birthday. She loves turtles.
Andrés:Ma'am, we can't send live animals! Have you been reading the postal regulations?
Camila:Uhm… no. But I have been feeding him extra food this week, so he'll be fine for two days.
Andrés:Two days?! How long have you been planning this?
Camila:Since last week. I have been so excited! My grandmother has been waiting for a turtle since she was ten.
Andrés:Ma'am, please take the turtle home. Or… take the bus. But you cannot send it by airmail.
Camila:Fine. Can I send this envelope with a photo of the turtle instead?
Andrés:…Yes. That will be $3.50.

USEFUL QUESTIONS FOR THE CLERK

  • What have you been putting in this box?
  • How long have you been keeping this at home?
  • Have you been reading the postal regulations?
  • Why have you been trying to send this by airmail?
  • Who have you been sending packages to lately?
  • Has anyone else been helping you with this?

USEFUL ANSWERS FOR THE CUSTOMER

  • I have been planning this for weeks.
  • My family has been waiting for this package.
  • I have been trying to be a good [daughter / friend / cousin].
  • It's been a really hard month, I need to send this.
  • I promise it's not dangerous — I have been checking everything.
