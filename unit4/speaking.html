Unit 4: Speaking Exercise ✨

Dreams & Ambitions  ·  Choose your format below

Option A — Pairs / Parejas

Make Your Dream Come True

You and your partner share your real and imaginary dreams. Ask and answer questions, use the Unreal Conditional to explore hypothetical situations, and use adjectives from the unit to describe how you would feel.

Tú y tu compañero/a comparten sus sueños reales e imaginarios. Usen el Condicional Irreal para explorar situaciones hipotéticas y adjetivos de la unidad para describir cómo se sentirían.

Grammar reminder / Recordatorio
Unreal Conditional:
If + Past Simple → would + base verb
  • "If I had more confidence, I would audition."
  • "If I were Cindy, I would be terrified."
  • "What would you do if you lost your job?"
I wish:
I wish + Past Simple
  • "I wish I had more self-confidence."
  • "I wish I could sing like Cindy."
  • "I wish I were more determined."
Step 1 Past dreams — when you were a child ~2 min
Ask your partner:
  • What was your dream when you were a child?
  • Did your dream come true? Why or why not?
  • Were you determined? Or did you give up?
Sentence starters:
  • When I was a child, I dreamed about…
  • My dream was to… but I assumed that…
  • I hoped that I would… / I wanted to…
Step 2 Present ambitions — right now ~2 min
Ask your partner:
  • What is your ambition for the future?
  • What are you doing NOW to make it come true?
  • Do you feel confident? Nervous? Determined?
Sentence starters:
  • My ambition is to… / My dream is to…
  • I'm studying / practicing / working because…
  • I know that I will… / I don't know if I…
Step 3 Imaginary situations — Unreal Conditional ~3 min · REQUIRED — at least 3 conditionals
Ask your partner:
  • If you could have any career, what would it be?
  • If you won the lottery tomorrow, what would you do first?
  • If you were Cindy, would you dare to try out?
  • If you could go back and change one decision, what would it be?
  • If you lost your job today, how would you feel?
How to answer:
  • If I could…, I would… because…
  • I would feel [adjective] if I…
  • I wish I could… but in reality…
  • If I were [character], I would probably…
  • Honestly, if that happened, I would…
Step 4 What makes a dream come true? ~1 min each
Discuss:
  • Talent / Self-confidence
  • Hard work / Practice
  • Support from family
  • Being determined — not giving up
  • A bit of luck
Useful phrases:
  • I think the most important thing is…
  • Without [X], I don't think you can…
  • If you are determined, you will…
  • Like Cindy in the story, if you…
Model example / Ejemplo — Step 3
Student A:
"If you could have any career, what would it be?"
Student B:
"If I could choose freely, I would be a musician. I wish I had studied music when I was younger. I think if I had been more determined as a child, I would have done it."
Student A:
"If you won the lottery, would you quit your studies and just do music?"
Student B:
"Honestly, if I won a lot of money, I would feel both excited and nervous. I would keep studying — but I would also record my own music. If I were Cindy, I would have been too embarrassed to try out. She was really brave."
Student A:
"I agree. If I were in her place, I would feel determined but terrified at the same time."

Notice: 5 Unreal Conditionals · I wish · adjectives: determined, excited, nervous, embarrassed, terrified · natural conversation

Option B — Individual / Solo

What Would You Do?

Choose 3 questions — one fun, one deep, one philosophical. Prepare your answers at home. Each answer should last 60–90 seconds and must use the Unreal Conditional.

Escoge 3 preguntas — una divertida, una profunda y una filosófica. Prepara tus respuestas en casa. Cada respuesta debe durar 60–90 segundos y usar el Condicional Irreal.

For EVERY answer / Para CADA respuesta:
  • "If [condition], I would [result] because…"
  • "I wish I could/were/had… because in reality…"
  • "I would feel [adjective] if… because…"
  • "If this happened to me, I think I would… but I also might…"

Structure for each answer / Estructura para cada respuesta

Use this for every question. Each answer: 60–90 seconds.

  1. State your answer clearly using an Unreal Conditional.
  2. Explain WHY — give at least one specific reason.
  3. Describe how you would feel — use an adjective from the unit.
  4. Add a consequence: "and if that happened, I would also…"
  5. End with: "I wish…" or "In reality, I think that…"
Model example / Ejemplo

Question chosen: Fun #2 — "If you woke up tomorrow with a superpower…"

"If I woke up tomorrow with a superpower, I would choose the ability to speak any language fluently. I know it might sound boring compared to flying or invisibility, but I think it would be the most powerful thing in the world. If I could speak every language, I would travel everywhere without feeling embarrassed. I would talk to anyone — a farmer in Japan, a scientist in Ethiopia, a child in a village in Colombia. I wish I could do that right now. I think I would feel overwhelmed at first — almost nervous — because suddenly the whole world would be open to me. But I would be determined to use it well. If I had that power, I would probably become a journalist or a diplomat. I wouldn't waste it on anything small."

Notice: 5 Unreal Conditionals · I wish · adjectives: embarrassed, overwhelmed, nervous, determined · clear 5-step structure
