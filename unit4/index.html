Use Unreal Conditional structures. / Usa estructuras del condicional irreal.

1. If Cindy more confidence, she would sing in public. (have)

2. If I the lottery, I would travel to Brazil. (win)

3. What would you do if you your job tomorrow? (lose)

4. If Jeremy the promotion, he wouldn't move to Brazil. (not get)

5. If I president, I would change many things. (be)

Your turn / Tu turno

Speaking task: If I could… / Si pudiera…

With a partner, ask and answer unreal conditional questions about dreams and ambitions. Use at least 3 unreal conditional sentences and 3 adjectives from the unit. Each person speaks for at least 3 minutes.

Con un/a compañero/a, pregunta y responde preguntas de condicional irreal sobre sueños y ambiciones. Usa al menos 3 condicionales irreales y 3 adjetivos de la unidad.

Starter questions / Preguntas de inicio:

If you could have any job, what would it be? If you won the lottery, what would you do first? If you were famous, how would your life change? If you could live anywhere, where would you go? If you could change one thing about your life, what would it be? If you had Jeremy's promotion, would you move to Brazil? If you were Cindy, would you dare to try out for the concert?
