Your turn / Tu turno

Speaking task / Tarea oral

In pairs: Student A asks for help in one of the situations below. Student B responds — agree, make a counter-suggestion, or politely decline. At the end, each student reports to the class what their partner said using Reported Speech.

En parejas: el Estudiante A pide ayuda en una de las situaciones. El Estudiante B responde — acepta, hace una contra-sugerencia o declina educadamente. Al final, cada estudiante le reporta a la clase lo que su pareja dijo usando Estilo Indirecto.

Choose a situation / Elige una situación:

📚 Asking a classmate to help with an assignment 🔧 Asking a neighbor to help fix something at home 🚗 Asking a friend for a ride to the airport 💰 Asking someone to lend you money until Friday

Must include / Debe incluir:

1 "asking for help" phrase 1 suggestion (How about…? / Why don't you…?) 1 reported speech sentence to report it A natural reason or explanation
