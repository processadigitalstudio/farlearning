The Coach and the Athlete

Pairs — minimum 1 minute / maximum 2 minutes

Rules

  • Student A = Coach / Student B = Athlete
  • Athlete picks a Scenario Card — press Shuffle for a random one
  • Coach must use at least 3 past conditionals (If you had... you would have...)
  • Athlete must respond, explain, and use at least 3 sports vocabulary words
  • Both must stay in role — this is a real post-game conversation
  • Timer starts when the coach speaks first — 2 minutes maximum

Example

CoachSo — what happened out there today?
AthleteI do not know, coach. I just ran out of energy in the final minutes.
CoachIf you had followed the nutrition plan, you would have had more stamina in the last quarter.
AthleteYou are right. I also think if we had warmed up properly, I would not have gotten that injury in the second half.
CoachIf you had pushed through those difficult training sessions last week, you would have been ready for this level of competition. But we learn and move forward.

The Press Conference

Individual — minimum 1 minute / maximum 2 minutes

Rules

  • Pick ONE athlete from the Cast Board below
  • You are facing the media after a loss or disappointing performance
  • Use at least 3 past conditionals (If we had... we would have...)
  • Use at least 3 sports vocabulary words from the unit
  • Follow the 4-part structure shown on screen
  • Speak confidently — you are a professional athlete, not a student in class

Example — Marcus speaks after losing the championship

MarcusGood evening. I know you all want to talk about today's defeat. And I understand. It was hard for all of us.
MarcusIf I am honest, if we had performed at our best level, we would not have lost that match. Our stamina in the second half was simply not there.
MarcusIf the team had been more disciplined during training last month, we would have been ready for this level of competition. That is on all of us, including me as captain.
MarcusBut I will tell you this: if we had given up after every difficult moment this season, we would not have made it this far. We will come back stronger. That is a promise.

Cast Board — Choose your athlete

Structure Guide

1
Open the press conference
approx. 15 seconds
Introduce yourself and acknowledge the result. Stay calm and professional.
2
Analyze what went wrong
approx. 40 seconds
Use past conditionals. What would have been different if the team had performed better?
3
Take responsibility
approx. 25 seconds
Use another past conditional. What should you or the team have done differently?
4
Close with confidence
approx. 20 seconds
End strong. What is next for the team? Show motivation and dedication.
