Past Conditional — Structure

El Past Conditional (Third Conditional) habla de situaciones hipoteticas en el pasado — cosas que no ocurrieron y su resultado imaginario.

If + past perfect → would have + past participle
  • If she had trained harder, she would have won the championship.
    Si hubiera entrenado mas duro, habria ganado el campeonato.
  • If he had followed the diet, he would have recovered faster.
    Si hubiera seguido la dieta, se habria recuperado mas rapido.
  • If they had worked as a team, they would have defeated the opponent.
    Si hubieran trabajado en equipo, habrian derrotado al oponente.
Error comun: no uses would en la clausula con if.
Incorrecto: If she would have trained...   Correcto: If she had trained...

Past Conditional — Negative Forms

Puedes negar cualquiera de las dos clausulas o ambas para cambiar el significado.

If + had not + past participle → would not have + past participle
  • If he had not given up, he would have achieved his goal.
    Si no hubiera rendido, habria logrado su objetivo.
  • If she had pushed through, she would not have lost the match.
    Si hubiera perseverado, no habria perdido el partido.
  • If they had not ignored the coach, the team would not have been defeated.
    Si no hubieran ignorado al entrenador, el equipo no habria sido derrotado.
Recuerda: had not se contrae como hadn't y would not como wouldn't have en el habla informal.

Mixed Sports Examples

Past Conditionals en contextos deportivos reales — practica identificarlos.

If + past perfect ... would have / would not have + past participle
  • If the athlete had warmed up properly, she would not have gotten injured.
    Si la atleta se hubiera calentado bien, no se habria lesionado.
  • If the team had had more stamina, they would have competed in the final.
    Si el equipo hubiera tenido mas resistencia, habria competido en la final.
  • If the coach had changed the strategy, the result would have been different.
    Si el entrenador hubiera cambiado la estrategia, el resultado habria sido diferente.
  • If she had not improved her nutrition, she would not have performed at that level.
    Si no hubiera mejorado su nutricion, no habria rendido a ese nivel.

TV interview — host and volleyball coach after losing the championship

HostCoach, your team was expected to win the championship this year. What happened?
CoachHonestly, we were not disciplined enough in the final weeks. If we had trained harder in the last month, we would have been ready for the pressure.
HostDo you think the injuries played a role?
CoachAbsolutely. If our key player had not gotten injured, we would have competed at a much higher level. But we cannot use that as an excuse.
HostWhat would you do differently next season?
CoachIf we had focused more on stamina and teamwork, the defeat would not have been so devastating. That is exactly what we are going to fix.

According to the coach, what would have happened if the key player had not gotten injured?

Two friends talking — one advises the other on working out

DanI ran ten miles yesterday and I can barely walk today. I think I overdid it.
MikeTen miles? On your first week back? If you had warmed up properly, you would not have felt this bad today.
DanI know. I was just so motivated. I wanted to get in shape fast.
MikeIf you had started with shorter distances, you would have built your endurance without the injury risk. You need to be patient with fitness.
DanWhat should I do now?
MikeRest and recover. If you had listened to your body from the start, you would not have pushed yourself so hard. Next time, set a goal and build up slowly.

What does Mike say Dan should have done differently?

At the gym — a man offers help to a woman lifting weights

MarkExcuse me, are you okay? That looked like a difficult lift.
SaraI am fine, thank you. I just lost my grip at the end.
MarkIf you had used wrist straps, you would have had a much better grip. It really helps with heavier weights.
SaraI did not know that. Do you think I should also adjust my technique?
MarkDefinitely. If you had kept your back straighter, you would have performed the lift more safely. Would you like me to show you?
SaraThat would be great, actually. I want to improve but I do not want to get injured.
MarkIf you had had a trainer from the beginning, you would not have developed those habits. But it is never too late to learn the right technique.

What does Mark say would have helped Sara during the lift?
