The Relationship Crisis Hotline

Pairs — minimum 1 minute / maximum 2 minutes

Rules

  • Student A = Caller / Student B = Counselor
  • The Caller picks a Situation Card — press Shuffle for a random one
  • The Counselor must use at least 3 past modals: should / could / might / may / must have
  • Both must use at least 2 sympathy expressions (That must have been so hard... / I am so sorry to hear that...)
  • Speak freely — you may glance at the card but do not read word for word
  • Timer starts when the Caller begins speaking — 2 minutes maximum

Example

Caller I do not know what to do. My boyfriend just said he wants to break up — by text message.
Counselor Oh no. That must have been such a shock. I am so sorry.
Caller It was. I keep wondering — did I do something wrong?
Counselor You should not have blamed yourself. He could have talked to you in person. You might have worked things out if he had been more honest.

Soap Opera Confessional

Individual — minimum 1 minute / maximum 2 minutes

Rules

  • Pick ONE character from the Cast Board below
  • Speak directly to the class — confessional style, as if talking to a camera
  • Use at least 3 past modals: should / could / might / may / must have
  • Follow the 4-part structure shown on screen
  • Do not read — notes are allowed but speak in full sentences
  • Timer starts when you say your character's name — minimum 1 minute

Example — Sandra speaks

Sandra Hi. My name is Sandra. I am 34 and I just found out my husband has been hiding things from me for years.
Sandra We met at work — he was charming, funny, everything I thought I wanted. But last week, everything changed.
Sandra Looking back... I should have paid more attention to the signs. He could have told me the truth months ago. I might have fixed things if I had known sooner. I must have been blind — everyone else saw it coming.
Sandra But you know what? I am not angry anymore. I could not have known what I did not know. And now I do. And that is enough.

Cast Board — Choose your character

Structure Guide

1
Who am I?
approximately 15 seconds
Introduce your character: name, age, and situation in one sentence.
2
What happened?
approximately 40 seconds
Tell the story. What went wrong? Use past tenses.
3
The regrets
approximately 40 seconds
Use at least 3 past modals. What should / could / might / must have happened differently?
4
What is next?
approximately 20 seconds
One sentence about your character's future. End with confidence.
