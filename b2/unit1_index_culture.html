Grammar – Past Modals

Select a modal to see the rule, formula, and examples. / Selecciona un modal para ver la regla.

should have / could have

should have = algo que era la decision correcta en el pasado, pero no ocurrio.
could have = algo que fue posible en el pasado, pero no sucedio.

Subject + should / could + have + past participle
  • She should have told him the truth earlier.
    Deberia haberle dicho la verdad antes.
  • He could have called instead of just disappearing.
    Pudo haberla llamado en vez de desaparecer.
  • They should not have broken up over something so small.
    No deberian haberse separado por algo tan pequeno.
Error comun: No uses el infinitivo simple despues de have.
Incorrecto: She should have tell him.   Correcto: She should have told him.

might have / may have

Usamos might have y may have para especular sobre algo que posiblemente ocurrio en el pasado. Expresan posibilidad, no certeza.

Subject + might / may + have + past participle
  • She might have left early because she was upset.
    Es posible que se haya ido temprano porque estaba molesta.
  • He may have misunderstood what you said.
    Puede que haya entendido mal lo que dijiste.
  • They might not have known about the wedding.
    Es posible que no hayan sabido de la boda.
Error comun: might have y may have son intercambiables en este contexto, pero nunca uses might solo para el pasado.
Incorrecto: She might knew.   Correcto: She might have known.

must have

Usamos must have para hacer una deduccion logica sobre algo que casi seguro ocurrio. Expresa certeza basada en evidencia.

Subject + must + have + past participle
  • She must have been heartbroken when he left.
    Debe haber tenido el corazon roto cuando el se fue.
  • He must have known about the divorce for months.
    Debe haber sabido del divorcio por meses.
  • They must have fallen in love very quickly.
    Deben haberse enamorado muy rapido.
Error comun: must have expresa deduccion, no obligacion. Para obligacion pasada usa had to.
Incorrecto: She must have study hard.   Correcto: She must have studied hard.

Dialogues – Unit 1

Read the dialogues. Past modals are highlighted. Answer the comprehension question after each one.

Nick Hey, Emily. I have been meaning to talk to you. Would you like to go out sometime?
Emily Oh! I did not expect that. You could have told me sooner, you know. I thought we were just friends.
Nick I know. I should have said something weeks ago, but I was not sure how you felt.
Emily Honestly, Nick, you might have missed your chance. I have been seeing someone for a month now.
Nick Really? I must have been the last person to notice. I guess I waited too long.
Emily Do not be too hard on yourself. Things might have worked out differently if the timing had been right.

Why does Emily say Nick might have missed his chance?

Laura Sandra, I need to tell you something. David and I are getting divorced.
Sandra Oh no, Laura. I am so sorry. That must have been a really difficult decision.
Laura It was. I think we should have talked more — communicated better. But we just grew apart.
Sandra I had no idea things were so bad. You could have called me earlier. I would have been there for you.
Laura I know. I should have reached out sooner. I just felt ashamed, I suppose.
Sandra Please do not feel that way. You must have felt so alone. I am here now, okay?

What does Sandra say she would have done if Laura had called earlier?

A Stranger From The Past — Final Episode

Victor Elena... after all these years. I should have found you sooner. I never stopped thinking about you.
Elena Victor. You must have known I would be here eventually. This town was always our place.
Victor I heard you got married. I might have come back sooner if I had known it was only temporary.
Elena You could have written. One letter, Victor. That is all it would have taken.
Victor I know. I should have been braver. I was afraid of what you might say.
Elena And what do you think I would have said?
Victor That you may have loved someone else. But maybe you never forgot me either.

Why did Victor not write to Elena?

Friends · Season 4 · Episode 1

"The One with the Jellyfish"

Ross and Rachel get back together — but only if Ross accepts all the blame for their breakup. He agrees without reading her 18-page letter. When he finally reads it, they argue again and break up for the second time. The episode is packed with relationship vocabulary: fault, blame, apologize, forgive, ultimatum, get back together, admit, argument, on a break, ex.

💬 Discuss with a partner
1Ross agreed without reading the letter. What should he have done differently?
2Rachel gave him an ultimatum. Do you think she could have handled it better? How?
3Were they really "on a break"? Who is more at fault? Use past modals to explain.
