Speaking task / Tarea oral

With a partner, role-play one of these situations. One person is in a dangerous situation; the other gives a warning. The first person ignores it at first, then accepts it. Use at least 2 warning phrases, 1 phrase to ignore, and 1 Past Perfect Progressive sentence to explain what had been happening.

Con un/a compañero/a, hagan un juego de rol. Una persona está en una situación peligrosa; la otra da una advertencia. La primera persona la ignora al principio, luego la acepta. Usen al menos 2 frases de advertencia, 1 frase para ignorarla, y 1 oración en Past Perfect Progressive para explicar qué había estado pasando.

Choose a scenario / Elige un escenario:

🚵 Someone cycling dangerously on a sidewalk 🏋️ Someone lifting a very heavy object alone 🧗 Someone climbing without proper equipment 📱 Someone texting while crossing the street
